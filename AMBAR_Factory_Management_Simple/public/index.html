Quick guide

Recommended daily workflow

1. Register each product once. The system assigns its AMB product ID.

2. Record workflow stages as work progresses.

3. When a product is completed, the completion entry also adds finished stock.

4. Record each sale to reduce stock. Record materials received and used/cut to maintain material balances.

5. Open Daily Reports for a date-specific summary.

How the report is calculated

Reports summarize saved transactions. Keep the records accurate and avoid entering the same movement twice.

Completed workflow entries automatically add the same quantity to finished stock. Do not separately add that same completion as “Produced” again, or stock will be counted twice. Sales and material usage must be recorded as their own movements.
